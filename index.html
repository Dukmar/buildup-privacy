Privacy Policy

BuildUp — Home Lifting & Workout Tracker
Last updated: March 17, 2026

The short version: BuildUp stores all your data on your device. We don't collect it, we don't send it anywhere, and we don't have access to it.

What Data BuildUp Stores

BuildUp stores the following information locally on your iPhone:

  • Workout sessions (exercises, sets, reps, weight, duration)
  • Exercise library and custom exercises you create
  • Workout templates and programs
  • Your equipment profile (what gear you have at home)
  • App preferences (weight unit, rest timer settings)

All of this data lives on your device using Apple's local storage framework. It is not uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

BuildUp can read and write workout data to Apple Health if you grant permission. This includes:

  • Read: Workout history, heart rate, and active energy burned
  • Write: Completed workouts

This integration is entirely optional. If you choose not to connect Apple Health, the app works without it. Health data stays within Apple's HealthKit framework on your device — BuildUp does not transmit it anywhere.

Data We Collect

None. BuildUp does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. There are no user accounts, no email addresses, no analytics, no crash reporting services, and no advertising.

Third-Party Services

BuildUp does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks. The app contains no tracking code of any kind.

The only external interaction is an optional button that opens a YouTube search for exercise demonstration videos. This opens in Safari or the YouTube app — BuildUp does not embed or track this activity.

Notifications

BuildUp may send local notifications to remind you if a workout has been paused or inactive for an extended period. These notifications are generated on your device and are not sent through any external service. You can disable them in your iPhone's notification settings at any time.

Data Export

BuildUp lets you export your workout data as a CSV file. When you export, the file is created on your device and shared through iOS's standard share sheet. BuildUp does not retain a copy or transmit the exported data.

Children's Privacy

BuildUp does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data leaves the device, there is nothing to collect.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date. Since BuildUp doesn't collect data, changes would most likely relate to new features that interact with external services.
